RENART

Which Renaissance master painted it?

Renart ranks which of ten painters most likely made a painting, from its style alone. A fine-tuned network is compared with a classical baseline on the same held-out images, and every number below comes from a committed, reproducible training run. It is not an authentication tool: style cannot tell a master from a good copy.
